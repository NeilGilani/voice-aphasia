> **(12–22s)** Stroke survivors with aphasia. People with Parkinson's, ALS, or a stutter. They know exactly what they mean, and the assistant built into every phone still doesn't understand them. About two million Americans live with aphasia alone.

> **(22–45s)** *[Live demo]* So we elevated the voice assistant. Same fragments into **VoiceOS**. On the call, Ellie's words show up simple, with pictures. It also reads his mail and runs his day.
